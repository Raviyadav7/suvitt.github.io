Frequently asked questions

Questions founders actually ask before signing up.

1

Do I need to replace my existing CA or CS?

Not necessarily. We can work alongside your existing CA and CS - or step in where there are gaps. Either way, we own the coordination.

2

How quickly can we get started?

One onboarding call. We map your setup, take over the workload, and are fully operational within a week.

3

What exactly does a Virtual CFO do?

We handle cash flow planning, burn tracking, financial projections, investor reporting, and fundraise prep - everything a full-time CFO would do, without the hire.

4

Is my financial data secure?

Everything runs on Microsoft 365 and SharePoint - enterprise-grade security, version-controlled, always backed up.

5

What if I only need one or two services?

No problem. We work with founders who need just compliance, just accounts, or the full stack. We'll scope it to what you actually need.
